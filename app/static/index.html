MAKE A LITTLE ROOM
Set your monthly budget
This amount carries forward to following months until you set a new budget. All recorded expenses, including subscriptions, count towards it.

  
ON REPEAT
Edit recurring payment
Use the full amount per renewal. Quarterly is every 3 months; annual is every 1 year. Short months use their last day. Schedule changes apply from today. Investments stay outside spending.

  
Delete this expense?
It will be removed from your totals. Deleted automatic charges will not be recreated. Future subscription renewals continue.

  
MAKE ROOM FOR WHAT MATTERS
Category budgets
Category limits are independent of the total budget. They carry forward until changed. Leave the amount blank to remove a limit from this month onward.

  
SOMETHING TO LOOK FORWARD TO
Add savings goal
These amounts are your manual savings records. They do not change expenditure or connect to a bank account.

Telegram notifications
Comma-separated percentages, from 1 to 100. Applies to the total budget and any category limits. Each threshold sends once per month; a jump past several sends one combined alert.
Reminders are sent after 09:00 Singapore time, within this many days of the configured billing date. The Telegram worker must be running.
