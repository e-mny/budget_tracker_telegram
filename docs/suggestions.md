# Feature review status

Approved and implemented:

1. Budget threshold alerts: configurable percentages for total and category budgets, with delivery history.
2. Category budgets: independent monthly limits, carry-forward behavior, and progress indicators.
3. CSV export/import: monthly/all-record exports, validated preview, atomic import, and duplicate detection.
4. Receipt capture: local OCR from dashboard uploads or Telegram photos, followed by review and confirmation.
5. Savings goals: targets, progress, target dates, and manually recorded contributions.
6. Month-to-month comparisons: total/category changes, matching elapsed days for incomplete months.
7. Subscription renewal reminders: configurable lead time, next billing dates, and annual commitment.
8. Travel currency support: original currency and amount alongside the SGD amount actually charged.

Still a suggestion, not implemented:

9. Split expenses / reimbursements: distinguish what you paid from your final share of a meal or group trip.

The GitHub image-publishing workflow and Proxmox update timer are also included. Enable the server timer once using the deployment guide.
