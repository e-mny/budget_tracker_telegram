# Feature ideas — awaiting review

These are suggestions only. None is implemented.

1. **Budget threshold alerts**: Telegram notifications at, for example, 50%, 80%, and 100% of your limit.
2. **Category budgets**: separate limits for food, shopping, travel, and other categories.
3. **CSV export/import**: spreadsheet analysis and importing existing expense history.
4. **Receipt capture**: submit a photo and review extracted merchant, amount, and category before saving.
5. **Savings goals**: visual progress towards a holiday, emergency fund, or major purchase.
6. **Month-to-month comparisons**: changes in total spending and individual categories.
7. **Subscription renewal reminders**: notification before a charge, with an annual commitment view.
8. **Travel currency support**: record the original currency plus the SGD amount actually charged.
9. **Split expenses / reimbursements**: distinguish what you paid from your final share of a meal or group trip.

Suggested first additions to review: budget threshold alerts, category budgets, and CSV export. They build directly on the core tracker without needing bank access.
